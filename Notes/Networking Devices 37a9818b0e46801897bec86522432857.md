# **Networking_______________________________**

## Definitions

**Network** : is a group of two or more computers that are connected to share information and resources such as data ,applications ,devices

**Wireless LAN Controllers (WLC) :** is a device that **centrally manages** all the WAPs in a network from **one single place**

**Router** :  always referred as default gate way , it connects at least two networks , could be a lan and the isp(internet service provider) network

or between two WANS . 

## Types of Networks_________________________________________________________________________________

| **Type** | Distance | Advantage | Connection way |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LAN ( Local area network ) | Small area (same office , same building) | High speed ,low cost and secure | Wifi or Ethernet cable, use switches to connect the devices within the LAN , and connect to the WAN ( internet ) via the router |
| WAN (wide area network ) | Large areas > cities , countries , or even globally ( Large size network ) | connects clevaland  AUH with clevaland usa | fiber optic cables |
| MAN (metro-politan network) | connects multiple LANs across a city or large geographical area using high-speed fiber-optic communication links. ( Medium size network ) | used for buildings that are close to each other geographically and to connect them ( like hospital) as it can connect the hospital with another hospitals on abu dhabi ex: cleveland , ssmc | Fiber optics cables |
| Campus Area network (CAN)
 | **LAN < CAN < MAN**  | used for Multiple Lans that are close to each geographically like campuses like SSMC between its buildings | between buildings> fiber optic, within the building > copprt |
| Personal area network (PAN ) | 4 to 6 meters |  | Using wireless technology such as wifi , bluetooth  |

**LAN** = One Building
**CAN** = One Campus
**MAN** = One City
**WAN** = One Country / Whole World

## Wirless LAN controllers

### Most Important Feature — Roaming

- Without WLC — when you move from **Floor 1 to Floor 2** your WiFi **disconnects and reconnects**
- With WLC — you move between floors and WiFi **stays connected seamlessly**

`Floor 1 📡 → Walking → Floor 2 📡 → Walking → Floor 3 📡
        WiFi NEVER disconnects! ✅`

### What is Roaming?

Roaming is when your device **automatically switches** from one WAP to another as you **move around** without you doing anything manually,

Local = Your phone uses your provider's towers 🏠
Roaming = Your phone borrows another country's towers ✈️
Cost = Roaming is always more expensive! 💰
